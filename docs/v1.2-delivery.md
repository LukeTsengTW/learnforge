## Grading behavior

New submissions use `ai-grading-v3` across all six question types. Single choice, multiple choice and true/false use deterministic grading. Fill uses blank/rule handling first and AI semantic judging for nonblank rule mismatches. Nonblank calculation and drawing answers receive server-side AI rubric grading with partial credit. Meaningfully blank calculation or rasterized drawing answers receive persisted system `unanswered` evidence and do not call the AI provider.

Formal grading uses `gpt-6-luna`, `reasoning.effort=medium` and `store=false`. The validated result contributes to the practice score but is only a learning reference and may be mistaken. Formal grading does not use personal Tutor credits; rubric provider calls are tracked in the private system ledger. Provider, rasterization or evidence-validation failures leave the attempt in draft so the submission can be retried. Result, History, Analytics and Mistakes rebuild v3 outcomes from persisted evidence without rerunning AI.

Historical `deterministic-v1` and `semantic-fill-v2` attempts retain their versioned interpretation. Older attempts are not regraded, and missing or corrupt v3 evidence is unavailable rather than inferred to mean unanswered.
